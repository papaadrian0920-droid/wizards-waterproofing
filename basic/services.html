Services

What Wizards does.

Seven kinds of work, inside the basement, outside it and under it. Not sure which one you need? That's what the free estimate is for.

01

Exterior waterproofing

The wall is dug out down to the footing and cleaned. Any cracks are sealed, then the wall gets a waterproof membrane and a dimpled drainage board.

New weeping tile goes in beside the footing, bedded in clear stone, and the soil goes back sloped away from the house.

02

Interior waterproofing

A channel is cut along the inside edge of the basement floor and a drain pipe goes in. It carries the water to a sump pump, and new concrete covers it.

It works well for water coming in where the floor meets the wall.

03

Foundation crack repair

Leaking cracks in the foundation are injected and sealed all the way through the wall, so water can't get in that way anymore.

04

Sump pumps

New sump pumps and replacements, with an alarm and a battery backup, so the water that collects in the pit gets pumped out before it floods.

A sump pump with an alarm can qualify for the City of Toronto rebate below.

05

Underpinning

Lowering the basement floor for real ceiling height, with an engineer's design and a City building permit.

06

Renovations

Once the basement is dry, finishing it into real living space.

07

Landscaping and grading

Sloping the ground away from the house, so rain runs off instead of soaking down by the foundation wall.
