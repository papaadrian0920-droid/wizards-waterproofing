How a dry basement works

Where the rain goes.

Rain off the roof goes down the downspout and out onto the lawn. Rain that soaks into the ground finds the weeping tile, drains into the sump pit, and the pump sends it back outside.

Questions

Straight answers.

QInside or outside waterproofing?

AOutside seals the wall itself and is the most complete fix. Inside means no digging in the yard, and works well for water coming in where the floor meets the wall. Michael looks first and tells you which one your house needs.

QIs the estimate really free?

AYes. He comes out, finds where the water is getting in, and gives you a price. No charge and no pressure.

QIt only leaks in spring. Should I wait?

ASpring melt is when most basements leak, and when everyone calls at once. Fixing it in fall or winter means it's done before the next melt.

QCan I just text a photo?

AYes. Text a photo of the wet spot to 416-569-0776 and say when it leaks: after rain, in spring, or all the time.
