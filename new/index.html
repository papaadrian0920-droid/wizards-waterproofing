What Wizards does

Inside, outside, underneath.

01

Exterior waterproofing

Dig to the footing, seal the wall, add a drainage board and new weeping tile. The most complete fix there is.

How it's done 02

Interior waterproofing

A drain along the inside edge of the floor that carries water to a sump pump. No digging up the yard.

How it's done 03

Foundation crack repair

Leaking cracks injected and sealed, so water stops coming through the wall.

How it's done 04

Sump pumps

New pumps and replacements, with an alarm and battery backup, so the water that collects gets pumped out.

How it's done 05

Underpinning

Lowering the basement floor for real ceiling height, with an engineer's design and a City permit.

How it's done 06

Renovations

Once the basement is dry, finishing it into real living space.

Landscaping and grading

Sloping the ground away from the house, so rain runs off instead of soaking down by the wall.
