Questions

Straight answers.

Inside or outside waterproofing?

Outside seals the wall itself and is the most complete fix. Inside means no digging in the yard, and works well for water coming in where the floor meets the wall. Michael looks first and tells you which one your house needs.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. He comes out, finds where the water is getting in, and gives you a price. No charge and no pressure.

It only leaks in spring. Should I wait?

Spring melt is when most basements leak, and when everyone calls at once. Fixing it in fall or winter means it's done before the next melt.

Can I just text a photo?

Yes. Text a photo of the wet spot to 416-569-0776 and say when it leaks: after rain, in spring, or all the time.
