How a leaking basement gets fixed.

This is exterior waterproofing, the most complete fix there is. Scroll, and watch the wall get fixed one step at a time.

01

Why it leaks

Rain and melting snow soak the soil around your house. That water pushes against the foundation and gets in through any crack it finds. The old weeping tile, the drain pipe beside the footing, fills up with silt over the years, so the water has nowhere else to go.

02

Dig down to the footing

The soil along the leaking wall gets dug out, all the way down to the footing the house sits on. It's the only way to see the whole outside of the wall and fix all of it.

03

Clean it, seal the cracks

The wall is cleaned off so every crack shows. The cracks get repaired, and the old clogged weeping tile comes out.

04

Waterproof membrane

A waterproof membrane goes on the outside of the wall, from the footing up to ground level. Water can't get through it.

05

Drainage board

A dimpled drainage board goes over the membrane. It keeps the soil off the wall and gives water an easy path straight down, instead of sitting against your foundation.

06

New weeping tile

A new perforated drain pipe goes in beside the footing, covered in clear stone. Water that runs down the board soaks into the pipe and gets carried away from the house.

07

Fill it in, slope it away

The trench is filled back in and the ground is sloped away from the house, so rain runs off instead of soaking down by the wall. That's a dry basement.

Every house is different. Sometimes a crack injection or a new sump pump does the job for a lot less. Michael will look first and tell you straight what yours needs.
