Questions

Good to know

Do I need interior or exterior waterproofing?

It depends on where the water comes in and what's around your house. Exterior seals the wall from the outside and is the most complete fix. Interior is less digging and works well for water coming in at the floor. We'll look first and tell you straight.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, find the problem and give you a written price. No charge and no pressure.

My basement only leaks in spring. Should I wait?

Spring snowmelt and heavy rain are when most basements leak, and they're also when everyone calls at once. Fixing it in the fall or winter means you're ready before it happens again.

My sump pump is over 10 years old. Is that a problem?

Most pumps don't last much longer than that, and they usually fail during a storm, when you need them most. A new pump with a battery backup is cheap compared to a flooded basement, and the City may cover part of it.

Can you help with the City of Toronto subsidy?

Ask us. We can tell you which work on your house is part of the program. The City handles the application and decides eligibility.
