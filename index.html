What we do

Inside, outside and underneath. We'll tell you what your house actually needs, even when it's the cheap fix.

Basement waterproofing, inside or out

Outside: we dig down to the footing, seal the foundation with a membrane and lay new weeping tile. Inside: a drain channel along the base of the walls carries water to a sump pump, with no digging up your garden.

Foundation crack repair

Cracks sealed from inside by injection, or from outside where the wall needs it.

Sump pumps

New pumps, replacements and battery backups so it keeps running when the power goes out.

Backwater valves

Stops city sewer water from coming up your floor drain in a big storm.

Underpinning

Lower the basement floor for real headroom, done safely under the walls.

Renovations

Once it's dry, finish it. Basements built to stay dry.

Landscaping and grading

Slope the yard away from the house so rain runs off instead of in.

Where we work

Good questions

Do I need interior or exterior waterproofing?

It depends where the water comes in and what's around your house. Outside seals the wall itself and is the most complete fix. Inside means less digging and works well for water coming in at the floor. We'll look first and tell you straight.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, find the problem and give you a written price. No charge and no pressure.

It only leaks in spring. Should I wait?

Spring is when most basements leak, and when everyone calls at once. Fixing it in the fall or winter means you're ready before it happens again.

My sump pump is over 10 years old. Is that bad?

Most don't last much longer, and they tend to fail in a storm. A new pump with a battery backup costs far less than a flooded basement, and the City may cover part of it.
